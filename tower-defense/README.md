# Study Defense

A simple educational 2D tower defense game. Open it on your phone or laptop in a browser.

## Play on your Mac

1. Copy this `tower-defense` folder to your Mac.
2. In Terminal: `cd tower-defense` then `python3 -m http.server 8080`
3. Open http://localhost:8080

You can also open `index.html` directly if Phaser’s CDN is allowed.

## How to play

1. Pick a tool (Pencil, Book, or Calc).
2. Tap a grass tile to place it. Path tiles are for enemies only.
3. Survive 8 waves. Correct bonus questions give extra gold.
